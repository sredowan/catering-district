# SEO Strategy — Catering District

Research phase. Audience, market, competitors, and keyword clusters, followed by a
proposed page architecture. No pages built yet — this document is the brief.

> **On search volumes:** this research has no Ahrefs / SEMrush / Google Keyword
> Planner access, so it contains **no invented monthly volume numbers**. Keywords are
> ranked by *commercial intent* and *evidence they are actively used* (live tenders,
> competitor page titles, industry body language). Before building, run the Priority
> Keyword list through Keyword Planner or Ahrefs to attach real volumes. Everything
> below is sourced and verifiable.

---

## 1. Who we are actually selling to

The buyer is **not a consumer looking for a caterer**. It is a small committee inside a
not-for-profit licensed club:

| Role | What they care about | What triggers the search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CEO / General Manager** | F&B margin, member satisfaction, board reporting | Bistro underperforming, contract expiring |
| **Board Directors** | Risk, compliance, member experience, cost | Annual review, audit finding, member complaints |
| **Operations Manager** | Rostering, food cost, kitchen consistency | Chef resigned, wage costs blowing out |
| **Company Secretary** | The EOI/tender process itself | Board resolved to go to market |

### Why they go looking

The economics are the story, and they are brutal:

- **~70% of clubs subsidise their F&B operation** — it runs at a loss and members' other
  spend covers it ([Club Benchmarking](https://www.clubbenchmarking.com/club-food-and-beverage))
- **Labour cost is ~32% higher in clubs than in public restaurants**, and profitable
  clubs run labour up to **27% lower** than unprofitable ones
  ([Club Benchmarking](https://www.clubbenchmarking.com/food-and-beverage))
- A club's customer base is **capped at members plus guests** — revenue can't be grown
  by simply attracting more people, so cost control is the only lever
- Wage costs continue rising post-COVID against Registered Clubs Award rates
  ([Fair Work MA000058](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/employment-conditions/awards/awards-summary/ma000058-summary))

**This is exactly Maz's pitch.** "Turning underperforming kitchens into profitable,
compliant, scalable operations" maps 1:1 onto the buyer's actual pain. The site should
lead with margin and compliance, not with food photography.

### How they buy

Clubs rarely hire a caterer by browsing. They run a formal process:

1. Board resolves to go to market
2. **EOI (Expression of Interest)** or **RFT (tender)** is advertised — on
   [Australian Tenders](https://www.australiantenders.com.au/), ClubsNSW channels,
   Club Management, or direct outreach
3. Operators are scored against selection criteria
4. Board reviews a recommendation report

Live examples found: Carrington Cove Bowling & Sports Club (bistro/restaurant EOI, 1,300
members), Lake Macquarie Yacht Club (restaurant, bistro, function & catering EOI),
Wangaratta RSL (bistro and catering licence), Forster Bowling Club (café and function
catering), Bankstown Sports Club / Auburn Tennis Club (contract caterer EOI).

**SEO implication:** the site must rank for the language of that process — *EOI, tender,
contract caterer, bistro operator* — and must be credible to someone who lands on it
holding a scoring matrix. A "Tenders & EOI" page is a must-have, not a nice-to-have.

## 2. The club landscape

Australian club industry composition ([ClubsNSW](https://www.clubsnsw.com.au/) /
[Chef Hire](https://www.chefhire.com.au/post/club-kitchen-staffing-australia/)):

| Club type | Share of industry | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bowling clubs** | 24% | **Highest** — biggest segment, heavy EOI activity |
| **Sporting & recreation clubs** | 24% | **Highest** |
| **Golf clubs** | 17% | **High** — functions + members dining |
| **RSL clubs** | 15% | **High** — Maz has direct RSL credentials |
| Community & workers clubs | 4% | Medium |
| Cultural & religious clubs | 4% | Medium |

NSW market size: **1,200+ registered clubs**, **54,000+ employees**, **6.7 million
memberships**, **$3.7 billion economic contribution**
([ClubsNSW](https://www.clubsnsw.com.au/about-us/organisation-information/corporate-profile)).

Full club taxonomy worth targeting, from the Registered Clubs categories and live EOIs:

- RSL and sub-branch clubs, memorial clubs
- **Leagues clubs** (rugby league) — e.g. North Sydney Leagues, Eastern Suburbs Leagues
- Bowling clubs ("bowlos")
- Golf and country clubs
- Sports and recreation clubs — football, rugby union, soccer, tennis, shooting
- **Surf life saving clubs** — large NSW coastal network
- **Yacht, motor yacht, sailing and rowing clubs** — e.g. Lake Macquarie Yacht Club, Rowers on Cooks River
- Workers clubs, community clubs
- Ethnic and cultural clubs
- Leisure / recreation groups (e.g. Mingara Leisure Group)

> **One term I could not verify: "bricks club."** It doesn't appear in any Australian
> club classification I searched. If you meant **leagues clubs**, that's covered above.

## 3. Competitor analysis

### Catering HQ — the direct competitor to beat
[cateringhq.com.au](https://www.cateringhq.com.au/)

**19 club venues**: Pittwater RSL, Castle Hill RSL, Cronulla RSL, Penrith RSL, Castle Hill
Bowling Club, Nelson Bay Bowling Club, Brighton Lakes Golf, Camden Golf Club, Lynwood
Country Club, Club Parramatta, Club Jervis Bay, Cronulla SLSC, Rowers on Cooks River,
plus Sydney Opera House and Powerhouse.

Structured as four pillars: Catering HQ (hospitality), Wholefoods (supply), Hospitality
Genie (profitability), Network (franchise). **They own this category's search space.**

*Gap to exploit:* their positioning is corporate and system-led. Catering District's
edge is a named, credentialed operator — Maz, JP, 19 years, Advanced Diploma, Quality
Auditing Diploma, HACCP auditor — who sits with the board. Lead with the operator.

### Maze Group
[mazegroup.com.au](https://mazegroup.com.au/our-venues/) — Northmead Bowling Club,
Nepean Rowers, plus own brands. Positioning: *"We build and operate hospitality brands."*
Venue partnerships + own food concepts. Thin on service-page SEO — few indexable pages.

### The Catering Group
[thecateringgroup.com.au](https://thecateringgroup.com.au/) — Lantern Club, Springwood
Sports Club, The Greens. Events-led with online ordering. Competes on functions, less on
whole-of-kitchen contracts.

### The Fresh Collective — **not a direct competitor**
[thefreshcollective.com.au](https://thefreshcollective.com.au/venues/sydney/) — corporate
and event venue catering (Art Gallery NSW, Luna Park, Overseas Passenger Terminal).
Different buyer entirely. Useful only as a model for venue-page structure.

### Future Food — **a channel, not a competitor**
[futurefood.com.au/eoi-tendering-management](https://futurefood.com.au/eoi-tendering-management)
Consultants who *run* the EOI process for clubs: campaign development, scoring matrix,
board recommendation report. They are promoted through
[ClubsNSW](https://www.clubsnsw.com.au/node/8493).

**They are a gatekeeper.** When they run a process, they decide which operators get
invited. Two actions: (1) rank for EOI terms so we surface alongside them, (2) get on
their radar as an invited operator. This is a relationship play, not just SEO.

## 4. Keyword clusters

Ordered by commercial value. **Cluster A and B are where deals are won** — low volume,
extremely high intent.

### A. Core service (money terms)
```
club catering                          club catering services
club catering companies                catering companies for clubs
contract catering                      contract caterer
contract catering companies Australia  club food and beverage management
club F&B management                    outsourced kitchen management
club kitchen management                club catering solutions
hospitality management for clubs       club bistro management
```

### B. Tender / EOI (the buying moment)
```
catering EOI                           expression of interest catering
catering tender                        club catering tender
contract caterer EOI                   bistro lease EOI
club bistro operator                   bistro operator wanted
catering contract club                 club catering RFT
respond to catering EOI                catering tender response
```
Proven live language — every phrase above appears in real club tender listings.

### C. Club type (the page-per-segment layer)
```
RSL club catering                      bowling club catering
golf club catering                     leagues club catering
sports club catering                   surf life saving club catering
yacht club catering                    rowing club catering
workers club catering                  community club catering
country club catering                  tennis club catering
```

### D. Location (multiply C by geography)
```
club catering Sydney                   club catering NSW
club catering Western Sydney           club catering Central Coast
club catering Newcastle                club catering Wollongong
club catering Hunter Valley            club catering Illawarra
```

### E. Problem-led content (reaches the CEO before the tender)
```
improve club F&B profitability         club food cost control
menu engineering for clubs             club kitchen labour costs
club bistro not profitable             HACCP compliance NSW clubs
NSW Food Authority club kitchen        Registered Clubs Award rostering
club kitchen audit checklist           how to run a profitable club bistro
club catering contract vs in-house     outsourcing club kitchen pros cons
```
This cluster is the long game: it earns links, builds topical authority, and reaches
decision-makers **months before** they go to market.

### F. Do not chase
```
catering Sydney         corporate catering        party catering
wedding catering        office catering           catering near me
```
These SERPs are saturated by consumer/corporate caterers — Catering Project, Feedwell,
The Catering Club, Fresh Collective. Wrong intent, wrong buyer, enormous competition.
Traffic from these terms will never become a club contract.

## 5. Proposed page architecture

Current site has **one** `/services` page covering everything. That's the core SEO
problem: one page cannot rank for twelve distinct commercial intents.

```
/services                          hub — links to all service pages
├── /services/club-catering                    ← Cluster A primary
├── /services/contract-catering                ← Cluster A primary
├── /services/kitchen-management               ← operations + compliance
├── /services/food-beverage-management         ← margin, menu engineering
├── /services/function-event-catering          ← weddings, functions, wakes
└── /services/staffing-training                ← rostering, team building

/clubs                             hub — "clubs we work with"
├── /clubs/rsl-club-catering                   ← Cluster C
├── /clubs/bowling-club-catering               ← biggest segment (24%)
├── /clubs/golf-club-catering
├── /clubs/leagues-club-catering
├── /clubs/sports-club-catering
├── /clubs/surf-life-saving-club-catering
└── /clubs/community-workers-club-catering

/tenders-eoi                       ← Cluster B. High-value, zero competition from
                                     caterers. "Considering an EOI for your club's
                                     catering? Here's how we respond and what we bring."

/insights                          ← Cluster E content hub
├── /insights/club-fb-profitability
├── /insights/menu-engineering-clubs
├── /insights/haccp-compliance-nsw-clubs
└── /insights/contract-vs-in-house-catering

/about/maz-islam                   ← already live, the trust asset
```

**Why this shape:** each page owns exactly one intent, internal links flow from
club-type pages into service pages, and `/tenders-eoi` catches the buyer at the moment
of decision. Every page should carry `LocalBusiness` + `Service` schema and a clear
"Request a capability statement" CTA — that's the B2B equivalent of a quote request.

## 6. Recommended next steps

1. **Validate volumes** — run Cluster A–D through Keyword Planner/Ahrefs. Expect low
   absolute numbers; that's fine. One club contract is worth six figures a year.
2. **Confirm service split** — the six service pages must match what Catering District
   actually sells. Needs your input before I write copy.
3. **Confirm geography** — NSW only, or national? Determines whether Cluster D is built.
4. **Build order:** `/tenders-eoi` first (highest intent, zero competition), then
   `/services/club-catering` and `/services/contract-catering`, then club-type pages
   ranked by industry share (bowling → sports → golf → RSL), then `/insights`.
5. **Capability statement PDF** as the gated asset — clubs expect one during an EOI.
